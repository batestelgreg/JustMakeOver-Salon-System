Purchase Orders

Approving a PO opens a supplier email draft. The system does not send email automatically: send the draft in your email app, then click “Mark as sent”. Printing is also available.

Supplier delivery check

Accepted items are handed to Receiving for RM processing. Receive accepted items first. When replacements arrive, use the same PO to record another delivery check for the outstanding quantity.

Outstanding delivery follow-ups

Refresh creates daily follow-up drafts for due POs with outstanding quantities. The system does not send reminders automatically. Open the draft, send it, then mark the follow-up as sent.
