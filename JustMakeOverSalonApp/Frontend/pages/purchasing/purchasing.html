Purchase Orders

Approving a PO prompts you to email it directly to the supplier. You can also click “Send PO” at any time to send the HTML Purchase Order via SMTP and track delivery status automatically. Printing is also available.

Supplier delivery check

Accepted items are handed to Receiving for RM processing. Receive accepted items first. When replacements arrive, use the same PO to record another delivery check for the outstanding quantity.

Outstanding delivery follow-ups

Refresh creates daily follow-up reminders for due POs with outstanding quantities. Click “Send follow-up” to email the reminder directly to the supplier, or mark it as sent if you followed up through another channel.
